The Hidden Cost: Inflation & Loss of Purchasing Power

Traditional pension systems are vulnerable to inflation and currency devaluation. Over time, the purchasing power of your savings can erode significantly, meaning the money you save today may buy much less when you retire.

For example, if you saved enough to buy a car and a piece of land in your early working years, that same amount might only buy a loaf of bread by the time you retire at 60+.
