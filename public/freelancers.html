Frequently Asked Questions

Blupension provides a flexible pension solution tailored for freelancers. You can contribute whenever it suits your income pattern, invest in a diversified portfolio including crypto assets, and manage everything through our intuitive platform.

There is no strict minimum contribution requirement. You can start with as little as $100 per month or contribute whenever you have extra income. Our platform is designed to accommodate the irregular income patterns typical of freelance work.

Your contributions are invested in a diversified portfolio that includes traditional assets (stocks, bonds) and cryptocurrency assets (BLU token, stablecoins). Our AI-driven system automatically rebalances your portfolio based on market conditions and your risk profile.

While Blupension is designed for long-term retirement savings, we understand that freelancers may face unexpected financial needs. You can withdraw funds early, subject to applicable fees and tax implications. We recommend speaking with our financial advisors before making early withdrawals.
